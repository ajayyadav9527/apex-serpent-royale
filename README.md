## ✨ Features (Mirrored from Orbit Merge & Upgraded)
1. **True 3D Three.js Battle Royale Engine**:
   - 10 active cyber serpents battling in a shrinking electric ring.
   - Dynamic logarithmic camera auto-zoom based on serpent mass & sprint FOV warp.
   - Floating 3D golden crown hovering over the #1 King serpent with a real-time HUD bounty compass.
2. **Procedural Web Audio Engine**:
   - Web Audio synthesizer generating dynamic sound effects: eating chime pops, turbo boost whooshes, killstreak fanfare, and elimination stings.
   - 🔊 SFX ON / 🔇 SFX OFF toggle switch synchronized across lobby and gameplay HUD.
3. **Cyber Progression & Missions**:
   - **Mission 1: Length Pioneer**: Grow your serpent to length 25 (+100 Credits).
   - **Mission 2: Apex Predator**: Eliminate 2 rival serpents in battle (+1 Revive Token).
   - **Mission 3: Storm Survivor**: Survive 90 seconds in the collapsing electric storm (+1 Magnet Pack).
   - **Mission 4: Quantum Harvester**: Collect 10 powerup capsules (+200 Credits).
   - **Mission 5: Victory Royale**: Claim Rank #1 in the arena (Permanently unlocks the legendary 🐉 VIP Golden Dragon Skin).
4. **Daily Cyber Supply**:
   - Free orbital airdrop claimable every 24 hours containing 1 Revive Token, 1 Magnet Booster, and 250 Quantum Credits.
5. **Booster Armory**:
   - **Quantum Revive Tokens**: Respawn immediately keeping 50% mass + 3.5s Phase Shield.
   - **Magnet Starter Pack**: 30-second automated energy orb attraction.
   - **Turbo Overdrive**: 50% slower sprint stamina drain for 3 matches.
6. **Mobile Touch & Desktop Controls**:
   - Responsive virtual touch joystick and boost button for iPhone, Android, and tablets.
   - Desktop keyboard (WASD / Arrow Keys + Space to boost) and mouse steering.
7. **Cross-Platform Social Sharing**:
   - 1-click dossier brag sharing to Telegram chats, WhatsApp, X, and native mobile shares.
